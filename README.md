# 🧭 Personal API

A short operating manual for working with me: how I communicate, how I prefer to collaborate, what I need from others, and where I try to be most useful.

This is not a contract or a personality test. It is a starting point for better collaboration. If something here is unclear, outdated, or not working for you, tell me.

## ⚡ TL;DR

- I prefer transparent, public-by-default collaboration.
- Slack is the best way to reach me. Email is not.
- I work remotely most of the time and am usually in the office on Tuesdays.
- I value preparation, context, and clear asks.
- I am happy to be a sounding board, give direction, make decisions, or take action — just tell me which one you need.
- I like async first, but I am not anti-meeting when a meeting is the right tool.

## 🤝 How to work with me

### 🧩 Default collaboration style

I work well with a mix of independent ownership and collaborative problem-solving. I like clear goals, visible progress, and direct communication.

I generally prefer:

- async updates before meetings
- shared channels over private conversations
- written context before decisions
- clear ownership and next steps
- decisions documented where others can find them

### 📍 Working location

I am remote most of the time. I am usually in the office once per week, typically on Tuesdays.

When I am in the office, I try to keep the day useful for in-person collaboration. If you want to meet in person, Tuesday is usually the right day.

### 🕘 Working hours

My usual working hours are **9:00 AM–5:00 PM Eastern**.

I keep several focus blocks on my calendar. If my calendar is blocked, assume I am trying to protect deep-work time unless something is genuinely urgent.

## 💬 Communication

### 📣 Best channel

Use **Slack**.

I prefer Slack because it keeps work visible, searchable, and easier to include others in. I am not a fan of email for day-to-day collaboration.

### 🌐 Public or private?

Public by default.

I prefer conversations in shared channels because transparency helps others learn, contribute, and understand why decisions were made. Use direct messages when the topic is sensitive, personal, or genuinely only relevant to the two of us.

### 🔄 Async expectations

Async is welcome. I usually get through Slack messages, threads, and mentions before the end of the workday.

If something needs a response sooner, make that explicit in the message.

### 📅 Meetings

I am fine with virtual meetings when they are useful. My calendar usually has some meeting availability.

Before booking time, please consider:

- Could this be handled async?
- What decision, input, or action do you need from me?
- Who actually needs to be in the room?
- What context should I read beforehand?

For in-person meetings, Tuesdays are usually best.

## 🚨 Interrupts and urgency

### ⏱️ Best time for quick questions

Earlier in the day is usually best, especially before my team huddle around **11:30 AM Eastern**.

### 🙋 How to interrupt me

For normal interrupts, tag me in Slack. If it is related to team work, use the relevant shared channel first.

For urgent issues:

1. Tag me in the appropriate public channel.
2. Escalate by DM if needed.
3. Use PagerDuty for genuinely urgent operational issues.

## 🎯 What I need from you

When you ask for my help, please bring enough context for me to be useful quickly.

Useful prompts look like:

- “I need a decision on X by Y.”
- “I need you as a sounding board for this approach.”
- “I need direction on which option to choose.”
- “I need you to take this action.”
- “I am blocked on X; here is what I tried.”

At minimum, please include:

- the goal
- the current state
- the specific ask
- any deadline or urgency
- links to relevant context
- what you have already tried or considered

## 🪞 Feedback

I am open to direct feedback, especially when it is specific and actionable.

Good feedback helps me understand:

- what happened
- the impact
- what you would prefer next time

I would rather hear about small friction early than discover later that something avoidable slowed the team down.

## 👥 1:1s

I am open to regular 1:1s. The cadence depends on the relationship, the work we share, and what would make the time useful.

I prefer 1:1s that have a purpose, even if that purpose is simply maintaining context and trust.

## 🌱 How to improve this API

If this Personal API is missing something, feels inaccurate, or could be more useful, open an issue or send a suggestion.

This document should evolve as my work, habits, and collaboration patterns evolve.
